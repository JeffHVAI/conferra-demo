Step 2 · Sealing and sovereign custody

What SkyeConnex holds for each graduate

CONFERRA fingerprints each document before it leaves the issuing service. SkyeConnex encrypts the package, erasure-codes it into shards on Canadian nodes and records issuance in an append-only Merkle log. The public only ever sees the custody reference.
